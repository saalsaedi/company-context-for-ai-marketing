# Stage 08 — Operations and team

Domain: operations_team  
Dependencies: 02 model; 03 delivery.

## Inputs
Workflow descriptions, service procedures, role ownership, vendor dependencies, capacity summaries.

## Review
Map customer delivery from intake to outcome, handoffs, support/escalation, recurring responsibilities and bottlenecks.

## Investigate
Ask for owners, realistic capacity, exceptions, dependencies and decision rights. Prefer role names over personal details.

Candidate questions (select only unanswered, useful questions):
- What happens after a customer buys?
- Who owns delivery, support and exception decisions?
- Which constraints or handoffs most often slow the work?

## Synthesize
Describe the current operating model, accountable roles, workflows and constraints. Separate aspirations to hire/automate from existing capability.

## Validate
No fabricated departments, guaranteed response times or staffing. Do not turn informal practice into approved policy.

## Approve
Owners review commitments and escalation rules; staffing changes remain proposals until accepted.

## Maturity adaptation
Idea: founder-owned proposed workflow. MVP: manual delivery/pilot constraints. Operating: process owners, documented service levels, vendors and capacity.

## Outputs
Operating context; role/decision map; workflow records; delivery risks.

Save records under domain=operations_team; preserve record IDs in all rendered views. Use [stage summary](../templates/stage-summary.md) and update the canonical progress state after meaningful changes.

## Exit gate
Ownership and delivery are understandable at actual maturity, with unresolved commitments visibly pending.

If blocked, log a gap type, affected claim/task, owner and next action. Continue independent draft work when possible. Review approval never changes evidence kind. See [stage engine](../references/adaptive-stage-engine.md) for transitions and [validation protocol](../references/validation-protocol.md) for release criteria.
