# Stage 10 — Governance and risks

Domain: governance_risks  
Dependencies: 00 scope; available domain context.

## Inputs
Stated jurisdictions, contracts/policies, risk register, ownership and approved disclosure boundaries.

## Review
Identify decision rights, material commercial/operational risks, privacy sensitivities, promises, and unresolved policy questions.

## Investigate
Ask which jurisdictions and commitments actually apply. Consult authoritative external material when a legal/regulatory claim is material and tools are available.

Candidate questions (choose one useful unanswered question; these are alternatives, not a questionnaire):
- Which commitments or risks should an AI assistant never overlook?
- Who can approve changes to pricing, policy and external claims?
- What information may be shared with which audience?

## Synthesize
Create risk records, decision owners, controls actually in place, unresolved reviews and context-sharing constraints.

## Validate
No compliance certification from a conversation. Proposed controls are not existing controls. Do not infer jurisdiction or approval authority.

## Approve
Authorized owner reviews policy choices and risk treatment; legal uncertainties remain bounded and routed appropriately.

## Maturity adaptation
Idea: proportionate constraints and pending choices. MVP: pilot obligations. Operating: existing controls, owners, review dates and documented exceptions.

## Outputs
Governance/risk context; decision rights; sensitivity rules; unresolved material reviews.

Save records under domain=governance_risks; preserve record IDs in all rendered views. Use [stage summary](../templates/stage-summary.md) and update the canonical progress state after meaningful changes.

## Exit gate
Material risks and disclosure boundaries have owners; uncertain obligations are not presented as established compliance.

If blocked, log a gap type, affected claim/task, owner and next action. Continue independent draft work when possible. Review approval never changes evidence kind.
