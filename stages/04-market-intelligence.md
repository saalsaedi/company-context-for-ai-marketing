# Stage 04 — Market intelligence

Domain: market_intelligence  
Dependencies: 01 foundation; 03 proposed/current offer.

## Inputs
Target geography, category definitions, competitor list, primary external sources, market hypotheses.

## Review
Separate the founder's market thesis from existing external evidence. Include substitutes and doing nothing, not only direct competitors.

## Investigate
Research specific uncertainties using dated primary sources. Capture competitor offer/pricing dates and comparable scope. Use a small purposeful source set.

Candidate questions (select only unanswered, useful questions):
- Which geography and customer category should the market comparison cover?
- What do potential customers use instead today?
- Which external uncertainty most affects the company's next decision?

## Synthesize
Create attributed research_evidence records, market hypotheses, competitor/alternative comparisons, and explicit gaps.

## Validate
No unsourced market size. Do not compare incomparable geographies, periods, customer segments or plan tiers. Repeated reporting of one study is one origin.

## Approve
Review strategic implications with the founder; approval of implications does not establish external claims as facts.

## Maturity adaptation
Idea: problem/category signals. MVP: alternatives and differentiation tests. Operating: changing competitors, distribution, segment shifts and dated constraints.

## Outputs
Scoped market map; competitor/alternative evidence; research gaps; strategic implications as proposals.

Save records under domain=market_intelligence; preserve record IDs in all rendered views. Use [stage summary](../templates/stage-summary.md) and update the canonical progress state after meaningful changes.

## Exit gate
Key claims have traceable scope and dates, or remain labeled hypotheses/unknowns. Research limitations are visible.

If blocked, log a gap type, affected claim/task, owner and next action. Continue independent draft work when possible. Review approval never changes evidence kind.
