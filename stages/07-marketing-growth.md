# Stage 07 — Marketing, sales and growth

Domain: marketing_growth  
Dependencies: 02 business model; 05 customers; 06 brand.

## Inputs
Channel plans, campaign summaries, funnel definitions, sales process, aggregated performance, experiment backlog.

## Review
Map acquisition, conversion, sales handoffs, retention and referrals. Extract actual metrics and attribution assumptions.

## Investigate
When operating data exists, resolve period, spend currency, conversion denominator, channel/source attribution and capacity constraints. Explain unfamiliar terms. For an idea with no users, skip performance questions and focus on one practical learning step; a simple plan to talk with likely customers is enough. Ask what growth decision needs context only if it is unresolved and relevant.

Candidate questions (choose one useful unanswered question; these are alternatives, not a questionnaire):
- Which channels currently bring customers, if any?
- What does a conversion mean in your business?
- Which growth experiment should the next AI task support?

## Synthesize
Separate active channels from proposed experiments. Capture growth priorities, sales motion, experiment hypotheses, learning metrics and owners.

## Validate
Targets are not actuals; last-click attribution is not proof of causation. No invented benchmarks or causal lift from before/after alone.

## Approve
Founder/owner reviews priorities and budget decisions; performance facts require data provenance.

## Maturity adaptation
Idea: learning plan. MVP: recruitment and signal quality. Launched: actual funnel with sample limits. Operating: channel economics, retention and accountable experiments.

## Outputs
Growth/sales context; metric dictionary; current channel evidence; experiment assumptions.

Save records under domain=marketing_growth; preserve record IDs in all rendered views. Use [stage summary](../templates/stage-summary.md) and update the canonical progress state after meaningful changes.

## Exit gate
An agent can distinguish the current growth system, targets and proposed experiments without inventing traction.

If blocked, log a gap type, affected claim/task, owner and next action. Continue independent draft work when possible. Review approval never changes evidence kind.
