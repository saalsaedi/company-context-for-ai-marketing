# Stage 05 — Customer intelligence

Domain: customer_intelligence  
Dependencies: 03 offer; current/proposed customer scope.

## Inputs
Founder segment choices, interviews, research notes, aggregate usage, support themes, anonymized reviews.

## Review
Distinguish user, buyer, beneficiary and decision-maker. Extract jobs, triggers, objections, desired outcomes and exclusions.

## Investigate
Look for direct customer evidence. Ask the founder about priority segments; do not treat adjacent competitor reviews as direct company-customer proof.

Candidate questions (choose one useful unanswered question; these are alternatives, not a questionnaire):
- Who experiences this problem?
- Who would decide to pay for a solution?
- What have potential customers actually told you or tried?
- How many people did you hear this from?
- Who is currently outside your intended audience?

## Synthesize
Produce a provisional or evidence-backed ICP, customer taste/preferences, journey, buying context, and segment-specific hypotheses with sample limits.

## Validate
A handful of interviews is not population prevalence. Persona details need a business purpose and supporting basis; avoid invented demographics.

## Approve
Founder approves target selection; empirical customer needs retain their own evidence status.

## Maturity adaptation
Idea: proto-ICP and tests. MVP: pilot observations. Launched: behavior and early retention patterns. Operating: segments, cohorts, churn and expansion evidence.

## Outputs
Customer records; ICP; customer taste profile; objections/jobs; evidence limits and next tests.

Save records under domain=customer_intelligence; preserve record IDs in all rendered views. Use [stage summary](../templates/stage-summary.md) and update the canonical progress state after meaningful changes.

## Exit gate
Target audience is either a reviewed choice or a clear open decision; observed behavior is separated from assumed preference.

If blocked, log a gap type, affected claim/task, owner and next action. Continue independent draft work when possible. Review approval never changes evidence kind.
