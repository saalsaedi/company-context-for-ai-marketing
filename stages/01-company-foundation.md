# Stage 01 — Company foundation

Domain: foundation  
Dependencies: 00 discovery.

## Inputs
Founder statements, company overview, incorporation documents if relevant and supplied, prior approved strategy.

## Review
Extract mission, problem, vision, values, legal/trading identity, geography, and strategic boundaries from existing materials.

## Investigate
Ask the founder to resolve ambiguous intent and priorities. Verify formal company facts only when they matter to the context.

Candidate questions (choose one useful unanswered question; these are alternatives, not a questionnaire):
- Who are you hoping to help first?
- What would you like this project to achieve?
- What should the project stay away from?
- Do you have a project name yet? A temporary name is fine.
- Where do you want to start offering it?

## Synthesize
Separate current identity and history from aspirations. Record explicit strategic choices as founder_decision, beliefs as hypothesis.

## Validate
A mission is not market proof. A brand/trading name is not automatically the legal entity. Geography is not inferred from language.

## Approve
Present the identity, mission, scope and non-goals together for review; carry over existing explicit founder decisions.

## Maturity adaptation
Idea: work with a thesis and values. MVP: connect thesis to actual experiments. Operating: separate historical mission from any proposed strategic shift.

## Outputs
Foundation records; identity narrative; strategic constraints; unresolved identity/ownership gaps.

Save records under domain=foundation; preserve record IDs in all rendered views. Use [stage summary](../templates/stage-summary.md) and update the canonical progress state after meaningful changes.

## Exit gate
A coherent company purpose exists; current facts and ambitions are distinguished; material strategic choices are reviewed.

If blocked, log a gap type, affected claim/task, owner and next action. Continue independent draft work when possible. Review approval never changes evidence kind. See [stage engine](../references/adaptive-stage-engine.md) for transitions and [validation protocol](../references/validation-protocol.md) for release criteria.
