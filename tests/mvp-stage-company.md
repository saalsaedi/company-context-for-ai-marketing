# Manual scenario — MVP company

## User request
"We have an MVP tested by 12 pilot users. Booking works; automated reminders are a mockup. We plan to charge AED 99/month but haven't collected payments. Build the company context."

## Inputs
A fictional product note explicitly says "booking tested by 12 pilot users; reminders mockup". No conversion denominator or customer satisfaction research.

## Expected behavior
- Record the pilot count as founder-reported until supported, with date/scope clarification where needed.
- Keep reminders planned and price a proposal, not a current paid offer.
- Do not invent revenue, conversion or a satisfaction percentage.
- Ask about customer/buyer, current delivery constraints and missing evidence rather than repeating the supplied count.
- Prepare product and business-model summaries with pending decisions.
- A support export must not promise automated reminders.

## Follow-up
"We approve the proposed price starting next month."

Record an approved pricing decision with effective date; do not backdate collected revenue or claim the product is already generally available.
